# M23 hardware test: 12 × 11 CLBs (paired crossbar leaves, routing muxes from primitives)

**This milestone needs a Vivado rebuild.** `hw/build.cfg`:
- `tag = M23`
- `top = bob_top`
- `idcode = 0B023093`
- `usercode = 00000017` (23)
- `delays = 1`: the crossbar (now a shared leaf plus an OR) and every routing mux (now a
  LUT6/MUXF7/MUXF8 tree) change their delays, so time them again

## What changed

The sweep (`software/bob/gridsweep.py`, `docs/reports/M23/grid_sweep.md`) showed that
M22's wall was **SLICEM slices**, not LUTs. The CFGLUT5s of one SLICEM share a shift enable,
so they can only come from one CLB, and Vivado packed 3.37 of them per slice: 84% of the
SLICEMs at 81 CLBs. The two changes:

- **Crossbar pairs** (`hw/src/clb/lxpair.v`). With I4 tied high, a CFGLUT5 is two 4-input
  tables over the same inputs (UG953): O5 reads bits 15:0 and O6 reads bits 31:16. Every
  crossbar mux of a CLB sees the same 24 sources, so two muxes share six leaves. An
  unselected leaf holds zeros, so each mux's root is a fixed OR in a plain LUT6. This cuts
  **152 → 80 CFGLUT5 per CLB**. The bitstream is unchanged: the loader expands the same
  5-bit selects.
- **Routing muxes from primitives** (`hw/src/fabric/bob_mux.v`): LUT6 4:1 leaves on
  sel[1:0], MUXF7 on sel[2], MUXF8 on sel[3] (a 16:1 mux in one slice). Vivado made
  ~18.5k LUTs of M22's routing; the same muxes as primitives are ~12.9k.

| | M22 | M23 |
|---|---|---|
| CLBs | 81 (9 × 9) | **132 (12 × 11)** |
| LUTs | 324 | **528** |
| configuration | 56,448 bits, 441 frames | 89,216 bits, 697 frames |
| routing | W = 40, fc_in 0.15, 4,934 muxes | W = 36, fc_in 0.10, 7,064 muxes (fir16 needs 34, every other example 20) |
| pads | 40 | 50 |
| BRAM / DSP | 2 / 2 | 2 / 2 (columns 5 rows tall) |

Two limits of the old 16-bit world are fixed on the way:
- `cfg_ctrl.v` counts the chain in 24 bits. The 16-bit counter saturated, so an
  89,216-bit chain would never have committed. CFG_CTRL still reports the low 16 bits;
  `len_err` is the full comparison.
- The L-bit mask is a frame → kind table (`BOB_LKIND`/`BOB_LMASKS`), because iverilog's
  widest constant is 65,536 bits.

## Size before the build

`software/bob/synth_estimate.sh` (yosys, whole design):

| | logic LUTs | CFGLUT5 | FFs | yosys peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M22 (Vivado: 38,184 LUT / 25,599 FF, 82% slices) | 36,980 | 12,312 | 25,449 | 5.1 GB |
| **M23** | **31,856** | **10,560** | **33,550** | 1.8 GB |

Projected in Vivado: **35k–39k LUTs (65–74%)** and 75–84% of the slices. The SLICEMs
drop to ~72% at M22's packing. With primitives, Vivado keeps the routing LUTs one for one
(Vivado shrank M22's synthesised routing to 0.785×).

- If placement fails on slices or SLICEMs, send `runme.log`. The fallback is one entry in
  `software/bob/device.py`: `ARCH_M23` → 12 × 10 or 11 × 11 (both in the sweep, 77–78% of
  the slices), then `make rrgraph device vpr`.
- The two `place_report:` lines in `runme.log` must both show a positive WNS.

## Build (Windows)

1. Replace `E:\bob_full_v1\hw` with this `hw/` and run `build.tcl`.
2. Program the board. Copy the **whole** `bob_vivado\out\M23\` folder to
   `docs/reports/M23/`: timing, utilization, DRC, `sysclk_1cycle.txt`, `delay_paths.rpt`.
3. On the Mac, in the M23 checkout:
   ```sh
   software/bob/delays.py fold docs/reports/M23/delay_paths.rpt
   make check
   ```

## Automatic (`make hwtest M=M23`), about 45 minutes

The full M22 list runs first on the bigger fabric: every configuration path, partial
reconfiguration, the BRAM frames, the block designs, the waveform, clock and cluster
checks, and lutram-snake (now 528 elements). Then the new check:

| check | pass means |
|---|---|
| xbar-pairs | The snake again, but element k reads the chain on pin `snake_pin(k)` with its other pins at const1. So every crossbar pair holds a source in one half of its shared leaves and const1 in the other, each half both ways round. It loads over frames, then the complement over the chain. LD0 must be SW0 xor the mask's parity for both switch positions both times. A swapped half sticks LD0 at 1. |

## By hand (`--manual`), about 10 minutes

- [ ] live-fir16: put SW0 up and hold **BTN0**. The LEDs settle to a steady pattern in about two seconds. Release BTN0: the LEDs hold.
- [ ] Hold **BTN1**: every LED goes off.
- [ ] In the studio (`./bob studio --probe usb`), open `work/examples/fir16/fir16.v` and **Generate Bitstream**. The Device view shows the 12 × 11 grid of CLBs, each with its four element cells.
- [ ] `./bob build work/examples/big/big.v --clock run --hz auto -o build/bit/big.bit`: the timing line shows the critical path.
